What sits behind the number

Five conditions the news usually leaves out.

  1. You need organised accounts (contabilidade organizada). The simplified regime does not qualify.
  2. 2025 costs count, as shown in the accounts. No invoices to collect — the figure comes from the books.
  3. It is a competition: applications are scored and there is a pass mark. Not everyone who applies gets the 30%.
  4. Jobs must be kept, and the activity and address stay unchanged for three years.
  5. Applications go only through Balcão dos Fundos. Registering the company there and the SME certificate take days — do them early, not on the last evening.

How to reach the pass mark depends on the company. That is worked out one company at a time, with its numbers.
