What Demis Hassabis reads — books with sources

founder of Google DeepMind, Nobel laureate. Collected from public interviews and writing: for each book, where Demis Hassabis named it himself.

Checked: November 2026 · Igor Kartuzov

The Culture series · Iain M. Banks

He read the first book, Consider Phlebas, while making the game Theme Park. He still calls the Culture the best depiction of a post-AGI future — and an optimistic one: humanity flourishes and heads for the stars.

Where he said it: The Ezra Klein Show, The New York Times, 11 July 2023 · source ↗ · found via ↗
Ender's Game · Orson Scott Card

The story of a gifted child tasked with saving humanity resonated so strongly that he asked his biographer to read it before their first interview.

Where he said it: Sebastian Mallaby’s biography — as reported in UnHerd’s review, April 2026 · source ↗
Foundation · Isaac Asimov

He called Foundation one of the best science-fiction series ever written.

Where he said it: Post by @demishassabis on X · source ↗ · found via ↗
Gödel, Escher, Bach · Douglas Hofstadter

The book brings together Gödel’s incompleteness theorem, Escher’s drawings and Bach’s fugues — and shows how they are all connected.

Where he said it: “Demis Hassabis: The interview”, Google DeepMind channel · source ↗ · found via ↗
