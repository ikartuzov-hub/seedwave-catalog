What Elon Musk reads — books with sources

founder of SpaceX and Tesla. Collected from public interviews and writing: for each book, where Elon Musk named it himself.

Checked: November 2026 · Igor Kartuzov

Foundation · Isaac Asimov

He wrote that the Foundation series and Asimov’s Zeroth Law were fundamental to creating SpaceX. He advises reading the books in the order they were written.

Where he said it: Post on Twitter, June 2018 — as reported by CNBC · source ↗
The Culture series · Iain M. Banks

He named SpaceX’s floating landing platforms — “Just Read the Instructions” and “Of Course I Still Love You” — after ships from the Culture.

Where he said it: Posts on Twitter, 23 January 2015 · source ↗
The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy · Douglas Adams

He read it as a teenager during an “existential crisis”. His takeaway: the question is often harder than the answer — phrase the question well and the answer follows.

Where he said it: Fresh Dialogues interview, February 2013 · source ↗
The Moon Is a Harsh Mistress · Robert A. Heinlein

He says he likes this Heinlein novel — about a lunar colony run by a computer that wakes up.

Where he said it: Esquire interview — as reported by Farnam Street · source ↗
The Lord of the Rings · J. R. R. Tolkien

He read it as a child. He explained that the heroes of the books he read always felt a duty to save the world.

Where he said it: The New Yorker, 2009 — as reported by TIME · source ↗
