What we remind you about

  • 🚗 Periodic inspection: at 4 years, then every 2 years until 8, then every year — in the month of first registration.
  • 💶 IUC road tax: in 2026, by the end of the registration month; from 2027, in April.
  • 🛡️ Insurance: a heads-up before renewal so you can compare prices.
  • 🔧 Service: the month before inspection — one garage visit for both.

Rules checked in October 2026. When in doubt, Portal das Finanças and IMT have the final word.
