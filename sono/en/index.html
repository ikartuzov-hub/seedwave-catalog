What Porta do Sono does

  • 🌙 Door: four questions by buttons — snoring, daytime sleepiness, blood pressure and weight, age — and a traffic light: fine · worth a visit · don't put it off.
  • 🩺 Show the doctor: a screen in Portuguese with your answers and a ready phrase. Just show your phone — no language barrier.
  • 🗺️ Route: through the SNS (family doctor → specialist) or private care in Madeira — only verified places, with the verification date.
  • 🔒 Private: nothing leaves your phone. No sign-up, no account, no data stored.

This is not medical advice — talk to your doctor.

🚨 Shortness of breath, chest pain or fainting right now — call 112.

No diagnoses, no percentages, no promises. Only whether it's worth going, to whom, and what to say.
