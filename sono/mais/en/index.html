Sleeping badly is not normal

Sleeping badly? There's a door for that.

Porta do Sono is a free page in five languages: four taps and you know whether to see a doctor about your sleep, which one in Madeira — and what to tell them.

🚪 Open the door

Who it's for

  • For people living in Madeira who don't speak Portuguese: the page speaks to the doctor for you.
  • For those who snore and «it's fine» — and for those sleeping next to them who no longer believe it.
  • For anyone waking up tired for months who never asked why.

How it works

  1. Four questions by buttons — nothing to type.
  2. A traffic light: fine · worth a visit · don't put it off — and one sentence why.
  3. «Show the doctor»: a screen in Portuguese with your answers. Just show your phone.
  4. «Where to go»: through the SNS or privately in Madeira — only verified places, with a date.

✨ With every answer a window lights up in the house. On the fourth, the door opens — and dawn breaks.

Not medical advice and not a validated test — guidance to take the first step.

🔒 Your answers stay on your phone — only an anonymous count leaves it: the traffic-light colour and the language. No sign-up, no account.
